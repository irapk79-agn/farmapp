💡 How to assign an owner to a block: Click the block number in the list below, then choose an owner from the Owner dropdown and click Save at the top or bottom of the page.
✨ Faster way for multiple blocks: tick the checkboxes, then choose "👤 Assign owner to selected blocks" from the Action menu at the top and click Go.
↕️ Tip: Click any column heading (like BLOCK NUMBER or OWNER) to sort the list. Click again to reverse the order.
